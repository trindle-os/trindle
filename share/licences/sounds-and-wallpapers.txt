Sounds and wallpapers

The two event sounds (open.flac and close.flac) are made by Trindle's own
program tools/gen-sounds.rs, as soft sine chimes. No recording or sample from
anyone else is in them.

Eight wallpapers, the pictures in the style of earlier painters, were made
for Trindle with AI image tools, with the Trindle mark in each. They are not
copies of any painting. The image tool's terms, checked on 19 October 2026,
do not claim ownership of what it makes and do not restrict commercial use.
The other wallpapers, still and moving, are made from nothing but the Trindle
mark.

All of them belong to the Trindle project and are under the GNU General Public
License, version 3 or any later version. The law on copyright in AI-made
images differs between countries, and Trindle does not promise that copyright
exists in the eight.
