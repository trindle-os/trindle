Trindle Sans 1.000

A renamed build of Inter 4.1 (https://github.com/rsms/inter), with the
pound sign taken from Radio Canada
(https://github.com/cbcrc/radiocanadafonts). Both are under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1: Inter's licence is OFL.txt and Radio Canada's is
OFL-RadioCanada.txt, both in this folder. Neither reserves a font name.

Changes from Inter: a capital I with short bars, a small l with a curl at
the foot, a one-storey a, a 1 with a shorter flag, Radio Canada's pound
sign, one optical size (Inter's text size), and weights 100 to 900 in one
variable file, upright and italic.

Trindle Sans is free to use, change and pass on under the same licence. It
may not be sold on its own.
